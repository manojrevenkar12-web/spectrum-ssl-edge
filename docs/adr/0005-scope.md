# 5. Scope: civil spectrum monitoring

Status: accepted

The methods here (self-supervised representations of I/Q, robustness and calibration under shift,
open-set recognition, integer inference on small hardware) are general signal-ML methods. This
repository applies them to civil spectrum monitoring: communications waveforms in a channel grid, the
work of a regulator's monitoring service finding interference and unlicensed transmitters. It does not
model radar emitters, threat libraries, direction finding, geolocation or jamming, and it does not
use military parameter sets.
